Getting started

  1. Open Audio Transcriber from its desktop shortcut or the Start menu.
  2. On first launch the app downloads every model: the English speech models first (Parakeet v2, the recommended one, then Whisper Small and Whisper Base), then the AI cleanup model (about 4.5 GB). Manage models shows progress; you can close it and downloads continue. An interrupted download continues where it left off.
  3. Once a speech model is ready, choose Import audio, or drag audio files onto the window.
  4. Read the result, then use Copy all or Export to save text, Markdown, subtitles, or data.

Python, Git, and a separate FFmpeg installation are unnecessary. MP3, M4A, and MP4 decoding is included.

Reading and correcting

Each lecture opens on its most finished version. The tabs above the text switch between Original (speech recognition output), Transcript (tidied by the AI), Notes, and Summary. On Original, click a line to move the audio there; Ctrl+Space plays and pauses.

Type in any tab to correct it; changes save on their own. Corrections to Original are saved in an Edited tab. On AI versions, Undo my edits brings back the AI's version. Ctrl + mouse wheel changes the text size.

AI cleanup

When the cleanup model finishes downloading, Medium cleanup and Mixed formatting turn on. The Lecture | Dictation switch next to Import audio sets how recordings are cleaned; flipping it re-cleans the selected lecture. Clean up and Format switch a lecture to another level or style (the style applies to Notes and Summary); the other note and summary styles are prepared in the background, so those switches are instant. Settings for new imports, models, and devices are under Settings. Transcription works without the cleanup model. Check AI text against the Original tab and the audio.

Selected models stay loaded while the app is open, including when minimized. Closing the app releases their memory. AI cleanup needs several GiB of RAM. Choose what is prepared under Settings → Prepare in the background, or turn it all off to reduce background work.

Managing lectures

Right-click a lecture to rename, export, or delete it. Deleted lectures stay in File → Trash for 30 days, where they can be restored.

Updates

Open Help → Check for updates, or wait for an update notification. Choose Update and reopen. The app downloads the update and waits for current work to finish before installing and reopening. Choose Later to postpone a pending update. No terminal commands are needed.

Automatic checks happen at most once a day and can be disabled in the update window. Internet access is needed for downloads and update checks; transcription works offline after setup.

Your library

Recordings, transcripts, settings, and models normally stay in %LOCALAPPDATA%\audio-transcriber. Updating and uninstalling preserve the library.

The installer is unsigned, so Windows may display an unknown-publisher warning.

Third-party notices and rebuild instructions are in the installed licenses folder. Compatible modified libraries can replace the bundled libraries.
